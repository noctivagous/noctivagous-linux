wireframe-editing-view-to-rendered-view-toggle

Sometimes it would be preferable on a computer screen
to type out the document in an unrendered or plain
wireframe kind of state that is in the form of the computer.  
So, if the document is to be printed on white paper in serif
type, in the computer program it may be edited in 
computer terminal type, a wireframe with sans-serif,
showing a little more than wireframe state, and then the user
can toggle to the view.  This will keep the entire GUI
consistent.  The text will be in the same location but
the editing of it will be on the side that fits the GUI.
This way the editing mode will look like the rest of
the GUI in the computing environment.
